Chapter 4

The road out of the valley was longer than anyone remembered it, and the wind carried the smell of rain across the fields.

She stopped at the milestone and turned the map over twice, as if the answer might be written on the back.

**A note in the margin:** *do not take the eastern path after dark.*

- The bridge is out.
- The ferry runs at dawn.
- Nobody crosses on the third day.

By the time the lamps were lit, the village had gone quiet, and the only sound was the river working its way south.
